Create one polished original web UI art-direction concept sheet, landscape 1536x1024, for a Chinese personal website called RYE. This is a visual proposal, not an implemented website. Show an aspirational but feasible web design, crisp typography and beautiful purposeful composition.

Core mood: standing on a gently sloping golden wheat field, looking UP into an enormous saturated clear blue sky. Wind, freedom, youthful curiosity, possibility. Combine the spacious layered scene feeling of Persona 3 Reload, the decisive graphic selection feedback of Persona 5, and the playful offset cut-paper label shapes of Dohna Dohna. Transform these inspirations into an original wheat-gold and cobalt-blue visual language. Do not copy any game's exact layout, logos, characters, icons or motifs. No red/black palette, no hot pink palette.
